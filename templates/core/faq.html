Ordering & stock

Are your products genuine and covered by warranty? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Yes. We source through authorised distributors, verify serial numbers on intake and include the manufacturer warranty stated on each product page. Your invoice is your warranty proof, so keep it safe.
Can I order without creating an account? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
You can browse and build a cart as a guest, but checkout requires an account with a verified email so we can send order updates and invoices. Your guest cart merges automatically when you sign in.
What does "low stock" mean? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
It means fewer than the product's reorder threshold remain. Stock is reserved transactionally at checkout, so the quantity you see is the quantity we can ship. If two customers check out simultaneously, the second order is safely rejected rather than oversold.
Can I cancel an order? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Yes — orders that are pending, confirmed or processing can be cancelled from My orders. Stock is released immediately and any captured payment is refunded to the original method within 5–7 business days.

Delivery & tracking

How fast is delivery? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Kathmandu Valley: 1–2 business days with standard delivery, or same-day for orders placed before 12:00 PM. Outside the valley: 3–7 business days depending on the district. Remote areas can take a little longer.
How much does delivery cost? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Standard delivery is free on orders over Rs. 10,000; below that a flat Rs. 250 applies. Express delivery is Rs. 500. The exact fee is always shown before you pay.
How do I track my order? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Open My orders and choose Track. Once dispatched, the page shows the courier and tracking number, and we email the same details to you.
What if my parcel arrives damaged? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Inspect the packaging on arrival and report transit damage within 24 hours with photos. We'll arrange a replacement or refund — please don't accept a visibly crushed box without noting it with the courier.

Payments & security

Which payment methods do you accept? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
eSewa, Khalti, direct bank transfer and cash on delivery. Online payments are verified server-side with the gateway before an order is confirmed.
Do you store my card or wallet details? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
No. We never receive or store card numbers, CVVs, wallet PINs or OTPs. Online payments happen on the gateway's own page; we keep only a non-sensitive transaction reference and verification result.
My payment was debited but the order says unpaid. {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Sometimes a gateway callback is delayed or interrupted. Don't pay twice — contact support with your order number and transaction reference. We reconcile it manually with the gateway and confirm or refund you.
Is cash on delivery available everywhere? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
COD is available on most orders and addresses. Some high-value items or remote districts may require prepayment; if so, we'll tell you before dispatch and never surprise you after the order.

Warranty & returns

What is your return window? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Unopened products in original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Opened electronics are eligible only for manufacturing defects under warranty. In-ear headphones that have been opened are non-returnable unless faulty.
How long do refunds take? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. Cash-on-delivery refunds are made by bank transfer, so we'll ask for account details securely through support.
Who handles warranty repairs? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Manufacturer-authorised service centres handle warranty repairs. We coordinate the handover, keep you updated and can arrange a loaner for some business devices. Physical or liquid damage is not covered.
Can I exchange a product? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Unopened items can usually be exchanged within 7 days for another product of equal or higher value, subject to stock. Contact support with your order number and the model you'd like instead.

Accounts & privacy

Why do I need to verify my email? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Verification protects your orders and ensures invoices and delivery updates reach the right person. You can browse with an unverified account, but checkout unlocks after verification. Resend the link from the verification page.
How do I reset my password? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. We email a single-use link that expires shortly. Support will never ask for your password.
How is my personal data used? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Only to process orders, provide support, prevent fraud and — with consent — send marketing. We don't sell data. Read the full privacy policy or email us to access, correct or delete your data.
How do I unsubscribe from marketing? {% icon "chevron-down" %}
Use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter email, or switch off marketing in Profile settings. You'll still receive essential order and security emails.
